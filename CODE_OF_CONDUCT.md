# Code of conduct

Aion is a small project and we want it to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and send changes
without it costing them their patience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful.  Disagree with the code, the design or the idea, not with the person.
- Assume good faith, and say it plainly when something is unclear instead of guessing at what someone meant.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people for whom English is not their first language.
- Give feedback that can be acted on, and take feedback on your own work the same way.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the issues, pull requests, discussions and every other space of the project, and to anyone who represents
it elsewhere.  The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and ban people who do not follow it.

To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately on GitHub (the owners of [aionlang/aion](https://github.com/aionlang/aion)),
or use the repository's private reporting if it is enabled.  Reports are kept confidential.
